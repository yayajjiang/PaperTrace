# Event Radar Curation / 活动雷达收录规范

Event Radar exists to create action **before** an event, deadline, or opportunity passes. It is not a calendar-shaped press-release archive.

活动雷达的目标是在活动、截止日期或机会过去之前促成行动，而不是把新闻稿做成日历。

## Coverage matrix / 覆盖矩阵

Every review cycle checks the intersection of:

- **Fields:** AI/CS (foundation models, agents, world models, robotics, vision, NLP, systems, security, HCI), biology/medicine, physics, mathematics/statistics, materials/chemistry, and social science.
- **Opportunity types:** conferences, workshops, hackathons, competitions, demos, exhibitions, summer schools, grants, scholarships, volunteer calls, and submission calls.
- **Regions:** China, Asia-Pacific, Europe, the Americas, global online events, and hybrid access.
- **Next actions:** submit, apply, register, request travel support, volunteer, form a team, or simply reserve the date.

每轮编辑都检查“领域 × 机会类型 × 地区 × 下一行动”的交叉，而不是从单个熟悉圈子向外随意添加。

## Admission bar / 收录门槛

An event enters the main feed only when:

1. an organizer, scholarly society, university, government agency, or canonical event site confirms it;
2. its date and location/format are public;
3. it offers current action value or is a major recurring event worth watching before the next cycle;
4. the audience and relevance can be stated without marketing language.

X, Xiaohongshu, newsletters, public accounts, and community posts are discovery channels. Dates and registration claims must be reconciled with the organizer page. Predatory or look-alike conferences are excluded; a familiar acronym is not enough.

## Freshness contract / 时效约定

- Cards display `verifiedAt` and link to the organizer.
- The daily build validates all dates and flags upcoming records not rechecked for 14 days.
- Items sort by the next actionable deadline first, then the event start.
- Passed events leave the main list automatically; important recurring events stay visible for 30 days as a next-cycle watch.
- Never invent a registration deadline. “Registration open” without a published close date is not represented as a dated deadline.

## Editorial rhythm / 编辑节奏

- Daily: structural deadline/freshness check during deployment.
- Twice weekly: official pages for deadlines within 30 days.
- Weekly: broad discovery pass across the full coverage matrix.
- Monthly: add the next 3–12 months of major recurring events and review geographic/field imbalance.

Community additions use the event issue template. A high-volume public calendar may help discovery, but canonical links and human-readable relevance remain required.
